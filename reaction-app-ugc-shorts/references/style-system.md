# Observed reference style system

## Shared production language

| Element | Pattern |
| --- | --- |
| Runtime | 7.8–12.0 seconds |
| Canvas | 1080x1920 vertical; 30 fps in two references, 60 fps in one |
| Structure | One continuous reaction shot; no cutaway or end card |
| Subject | One centered, expressive person, framed chest-up/waist-up |
| Caption | Fixed upper-center, 2–4 lines, white bold sans with black outline, mostly lowercase |
| Product role | Product/app name appears once in the setup line; no feature walkthrough |
| Comedy | Familiar behavior plus an understated, readable reaction or environmental escalation |
| Ending | Hold the reaction long enough to land; no direct CTA |

## Caption implementation

Place the text around 12–22% down from the top, centered. Keep a 5–7% left/right margin and enough clearance for the subject's head. Use a bold, neutral sans-serif with high contrast (white fill, black 3–6 px outline at 1080px). Keep the copy stable rather than karaoke-style word highlighting.

## Reference-derived examples

- `me acting like i know my macros so i just open calai.app and let it handle it`
- `when you're just eating chill but calai.app casually drops your macros anyway`
- `me when i'm still logging calories manually instead of using calai.app`

These demonstrate the sentence rhythm only. Rewrite the joke for the actual product and do not reuse them verbatim.

## Selection rubric

Score a candidate from 1–5 on each dimension:

1. **Instant read:** premise and face are understood without audio.
2. **Human believability:** natural gaze, hands, lighting, motion, and background.
3. **Meme restraint:** one concise joke with no advertising clutter.
4. **Brand fit:** the product genuinely resolves the implied friction.
5. **Mobile legibility:** text and expression remain clear at a small size.

Use the highest total. Reject any candidate with a 1 in human believability or mobile legibility.
